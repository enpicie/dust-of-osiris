# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primarily the bot's owner (enpicie), checking the exact matchmaking-role rules
while answering appeals or changing requirements. Secondarily, players in the
MBAACC Discord community who want to see what each role takes. They arrive
from a link, look up one tier, and leave; the job is "what exactly qualifies
for this role?"

## Product Purpose
A single reference page for the dust-of-osiris matchmaking roles (Novice,
Fighter, Ascended, Elite): every requirement, exactly as the bot applies it.
It is generated from the bot's rules file, so it is always current. Success is
answering a rules question in a few seconds without opening the code.

## Brand Personality
Moonlit, arcane, precise. A quiet nod to Melty Blood's night-time atmosphere,
kept restrained: the mood lives in color and type, never at the cost of
reading speed. Calm and exact, not hype.

## Anti-references
- SaaS landing pages: hero sections, feature-card grids, call-to-action buttons.
- Wiki walls of text: dense, unstructured paragraphs with no hierarchy.

## Design Principles
- Exact over approximate: show the real thresholds (placements, entrant counts,
  dates, events) the bot uses, not paraphrases.
- One source of truth: the page is built from the rules, never hand-maintained.
- Scan first: the four tiers are visible at a glance; detail opens on demand.
- Atmosphere serves legibility: mood is restrained so the rules stay easy to read.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body text contrast, keyboard-operable collapsibles (native
`<details>`), visible focus, honors `prefers-reduced-motion`, readable on phones.
