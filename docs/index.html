Novice Starting role. No result at the Fighter bar yet.

Every player whose MBAACC results on start.gg do not yet include 2+ set wins (2-2 or better, byes excluded) in a bracket of 24+ entrants. Players with no MBAACC results land here too.

Fighter 2-2 or better in a bracket of 24+. Open to anyone.

At least one MBAACC result with 2+ set wins (2-2 or better, byes excluded) in a bracket of 24+ entrants.

Fighter is also the general looking-for-games role: anyone can take it, alongside any other matchmaking role.

How results are read

  • Only MBAACC events on start.gg count.
  • Top N means placing Nth or better. start.gg lists tied places as the best place in the tie, so 9th–12th shows as 9th and counts for Top 12.
  • Series (Midweek Melting, TNS, Melt Your Blood) count any edition's main bracket; redemption brackets do not count.
  • Frosty Faustings 2026 placements are read from its Top 8 bracket, because its event standings on start.gg are wrong.
  • Roles only move up. A newer, weaker result never removes a role you earned.
